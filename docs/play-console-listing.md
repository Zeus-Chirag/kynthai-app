## Full Description (4000 chars max)
```
Kynthai is a family health companion that helps you manage medications, track health, and care for the people you love — all in one app.

FOR FAMILIES
• Add up to 4 family members — kids, parents, partners
• Shared medication reminders with family alerts when a dose is missed
• One-tap SOS emergency button for seniors
• Large text and simple screens designed for all ages

SMART MEDICATION MANAGEMENT
• Snap a prescription label — we extract the schedule automatically
• Drug interaction checks before you take anything
• Stock tracking so you never run out
• Morning, afternoon, and evening reminder windows

AI HEALTH ASSISTANT
• Ask questions about medications, side effects, and general health topics
• Get help understanding lab results and prescription labels
• AI-powered insights based on your health data
• Always informational — your doctor makes the real decisions

DOCTOR & LAB ACCESS
• Book video consultations with US-licensed doctors
• Schedule home lab tests with verified partners
• View results directly in the app
• Share health data with your care team

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Your health data is encrypted in transit and at rest
• Stored on US servers — never sold to third parties
• HIPAA-aligned privacy practices
• Delete your data anytime

FREE TO START
Kynthai Free includes 2 family members, smart reminders, drug interaction checks, and AI health assistant. Upgrade to Plus ($9.99/mo) or Family Pro ($19.99/mo) for more features.

Kynthai gives health information, not a diagnosis. Always follow your doctor and the label.
```
