# PATIENT RIGHTS STATEMENT

KYNTHA HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES
Effective Date: July 13, 2026

---

Kynthai Health Technologies is committed to providing a respectful, safe, and transparent experience for all users of the Kynthai platform. As a platform user - whether as a patient, family member, caretaker, or designee - you have the following rights:

---

## 1. Nondiscrimination

You have the right to receive services without discrimination on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, genetic information, or ability to pay.

## 2. Information About Your Rights

You have the right to be informed of your rights and responsibilities as a platform user in a language you can understand. This statement and the HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices are available at kynthai.app/patient-rights and kynthai.app/privacy-practices.

## 3. Participation in Care Decisions

You have the right to participate in decisions regarding your healthcare, including consenting to or declining services, consultation with a qualified healthcare professional before making health decisions, and access to all reasonable information to make informed choices. AI-generated content on Kynthai is advisory only and does not constitute medical advice.

## 4. Privacy and Confidentiality

You have the right to privacy and confidentiality of your health information. PHI will only be shared for TPO purposes or with your explicit written authorization. You may request restrictions on certain disclosures and confidential communications as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices at kynthai.app/privacy-practices.

## 5. Access to Your Health Information

You have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of your health information maintained by Kynthai. You may request a data export at any time via Profile > Data Export or by emailing privacy@kynthai.app. Requests will be fulfilled within 30 days.

## 6. Right to Amend Your Records

You have the right to request amendment of inaccurate or incomplete PHI. Submit requests via Profile > Settings or email privacy@kynthai.app with subject: PHI Amendment Request.

## 7. Right to an Accounting of Disclosures

You have the right to request an accounting of disclosures of your PHI made by Kynthai for the six years preceding your request. Submit to privacy@kynthai.app.

## 8. Right to File a Complaint

You have the right to file a complaint with Kynthai or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights without retaliation. Contact Kynthai Privacy Officer at privacy@kynthai.app or HHS OCR at hhs.gov/ocr/privacy.

## 9. Right to Restrict Disclosures

You have the right to request restrictions on uses and disclosures of your PHI for TPO. Requests must be submitted in writing to privacy@kynthai.app.

## 10. Right to Choose Communications

You have the right to request that Kynthai communicate with you by alternative means or at alternative locations. We will accommodate all reasonable requests submitted in writing.

## 11. Right to Opt Out of Marketing

You have the right to opt out of receiving marketing communications from Kynthai. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or update notification preferences in your Profile.

## 12. Right to Be Free from Retaliation

You will not be retaliated against for exercising any of these rights or for filing a complaint about our privacy practices.
